Bergensers CommonsCall recap6 September 2026

Where we landed

Four regions instead of five, a year of renting before anyone buys, and a legal shell to hold the whole thing. None of it is decided yet.

Three things changed on this call

01 / The map

Four regions, not five

Norway, the French Alps, the Italian Alps, the Pyrenees. The German and Austrian Alps come off the list for now, mostly on price. At roughly 600k to 1.2M they were running four to five times the Pyrenees.

02 / The structure

An entity to hold it

Jesse and Asta both arrived at the same idea independently: set something up that can take a loan, sign contracts, and handle people joining or leaving. Business or non-profit is still open.

03 / The order

Rent first, buy later

Live together somewhere for a year before any money goes into land. This was the strongest idea in the room. It is a proposal, not a decision, and it deserves proper argument.

The triangle, with the four regions in it

The big three questions

Structure

Most of us want to live there full time. Jesse, Leon and I all said so. Asta most likely will not, at least not outside Norway, and that makes the full time and part time balance the hardest thing on the list rather than a detail to sort later.

On the physical shape we are closer than I expected. Everyone described some version of the same thing: a common house to gather in, private space to retreat to, whether that is a separate house or a small cabin. Asta wants separate gardens in proximity, which is what her parents built with their friends on an island in Norway. I want guest rooms so people can come without moving.

Still untouched: borrowing between us, buying in, and buying out.

Location

More agreement than anyone expected on the feeling, which several people put roughly as: it does not matter where we are, as long as we are together.

Much less agreement on distance to a city. Jesse wants an hour at the outside, ideally 45 minutes, with some transit and a central point. Asta wants a geographical border between her and the city, which is how she grew up, on an island in Norway. Leon pointed out that around Grenoble may simply beat Bavaria on price. Nobody has said which city, and that question is doing a lot of quiet work.

Timeline

The thinnest of the three. The trial year is the only concrete proposal we have, and the permanent commitment is the part that matters most: properly moving somewhere, not visiting it. How we see that someone is committed, and putting in real effort, is still an open question with no answer yet.

Is it special because we are special, or because the one visit a year is special?

What is still open

Asta and Norway

Three of our four regions are not Norway, and Asta most likely will not move out of Norway full time. So for three of the four options, full time versus part time is not a balance we get to design. It is already settled for at least one of us. Worth saying out loud before the presentations rather than after.

Climate change and southern Europe

How much do we want to put into the south. The Alps have warmed roughly twice as fast as the northern hemisphere average over the last century, and about half a degree per decade since the 1980s, which shifts the conditions we would be buying into some 100 metres uphill every ten years. Drier summers on top of that. Over thirty years this is not a footnote, and it cuts against the two cheapest regions. I will cover it properly in the Pyrenees section rather than leave it hanging.

What visitors pay

If some of us put money into a large plot, what does someone pay to come and stay. Rent of a kind, plus electricity, possibly a share of renovations. Nobody has put a number on any of it.

Renovations

Raised from a renovation one of our families has been through. Renovation typically adds 30 to 100 percent on top of the purchase price, cheapest in France and dearest in Norway. That is large enough to reorder the whole triangle.

How we vote

We joked about Plato, but a group buying property together does need a way to decide things, including how someone leaves. Probably a question for whoever drafts the entity.

Loans

Leon's question is the practical blocker. I'm presuming the answer differs by country, since lending rules do, which is another reason the entity question probably cannot be settled before the location one.
